Game Mechanics

1. Playable up to 4 players. Race to Cell 100 (The Golden Arrow).

2. Special Cells exist to aid or hinder your journey:

  • Orange (The World) - Miss 2 turns
  • Red (Star Platinum) - +2 extra rolls
  • Blue (The Hand) - Move 3 cells backward
  • Green (Echoes) - +1 extra roll
  • Purple (Hermit Purple) - Move 5 cells forward
  • Yellow (Gold Exp.) - Teleport to Cell 49
  • Pink (Crazy Diamond) - Teleport to Cell 56
  • Violet (Killer Queen) - Teleport to Cell 2
  • Yellow-Green (Sticky Fingers) - Move 8 cells forward
  • Orange-Yellow (The Fool) - Miss 1 turn
  • Red-Green (Heaven's Door) - Steal next player's roll
  • Blue-Red (H2U) - Give next player an extra roll

3. Exact roll needed to land on 100. If you roll over, you bounce back!
